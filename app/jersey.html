Practice range used by kit printers: back 8–12″, names 2–3″, front 4–6″. Zoom in the studio is a magnifying glass: 10 cm on the sheet is 100 mm on the plotter at 50% or 400%.

Fonts that weed on HTV

Numbers need mass: Anton, Tourney, Black Ops One, Russo One, Squada One. Long African names need condensed: Oswald, Barlow Condensed, Teko, Bebas Neue. Digital-watch / PE bibs: Share Tech Mono, Iceland, VT323. Thin scripts (Allura, Playfair, Gloria Hallelujah) are not in the picker — they snap on cheap vinyl.

How to cut a nameset

  1. Open the studio.
  2. Kit → Football / rugby — adult (or youth).
  3. Paste OTIENO,10 or load the coach CSV.
  4. Name font + number font + vinyl colours.
  5. Place kit, nest, mirror for press, export colour jobs or send HPGL.
